# Governance centre

Retain the approved Manrope/Newsreader typography and green palette: forest #14382d, pale green #e4ecdf, paper #f5f6ef, white #ffffff, ink #183b30. Use forest headings and left-aligned reading text.

The hub is a policy directory, not an article feed. Policy pages have a compact title, scope/contact summary, contents rail and addressable sections. Privacy uses a data-use table; grievances use a reporting sequence; equality uses clear standards; security uses an access-decision sequence. Native disclosure controls hold secondary guidance. No stock photos or decorative animation on reporting/legal pages.

Layout: [title + scope] / [contents rail | sections, matrices and reporting actions] / [related policies]. On phones the rail becomes an in-flow contents disclosure, tables become labelled stacked rows and email links wrap.

Review: consistent navigation is useful for policies, but repeating the same article layout would fail the brief. Use four content treatments within the common policy shell. Keep every essential clause visible without requiring an accordion. Avoid claims of certifications, universally implemented controls, guaranteed confidentiality, fixed response SLAs, or regulatory permissions without evidence.

Sources: three spreadsheets in Downloads/softaskwebsitework inspected as reference data. Mailboxes confirmed active by owner supersede workbook setup status. Restricted workforce/ethics inboxes are used only on their relevant reporting pages, not dumped into a general directory. DPO appointment, website controller and retention schedule still require facts; do not infer appointment from an active mailbox. Capability matrices are not evidence of customer permission or regulatory authorisation.

Primary guidance consulted: PDPC Data Protection Obligations; TAFEP Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices; NIST SP 800-207. These inform wording and do not certify Soft Task compliance.
