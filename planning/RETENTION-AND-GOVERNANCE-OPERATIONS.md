# Proposed retention schedule and operational ownership

Prepared at the owner's request, 15 September 2026. Proposal, not evidence of implemented deletion or final company adoption.

| Information | Proposed retention trigger and period | Responsible function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsuccessful project enquiries | Delete or anonymise 12 months after the last meaningful contact if no engagement follows | Customer operations |
| Unsuccessful recruitment applications | Delete 6 months after the recruitment process closes; a separate talent-pool purpose requires its own notice and opt-in period | HR |
| Active client and contract records | Move into the engagement record schedule; determine applicable contract, statutory and dispute requirements rather than applying the enquiry period | Legal and delivery |
| Newsletter records | Retain while subscribed; remove active subscription on unsubscribe. Determine any minimal suppression/consent evidence separately before adding a campaign provider | Communications and privacy |
| Pending newsletter confirmations | Current code: link expires after 24 hours; daily maintenance removes unconfirmed pending records more than 7 days after expiry | Website operations |
| Grievances, whistleblowing and investigations | A separate restricted case schedule, set by applicable employment, investigation and legal requirements; do not apply a generic enquiry deletion rule | HR / ethics / legal |
| Security reports | Keep for assessment, remediation and any continuing legal or operational need; define a case schedule by severity and purpose | Security |
| Browser cookie choice | Current code: up to 180 days in the visitor's browser | Website operations |

Legal holds override routine deletion where necessary. Review records before deletion; remove duplicates and unnecessary attachments across relevant inboxes and systems. A published schedule is not enough: Google Workspace retention/Vault rules, provider logs, exports and backups need an implementation and verification plan. No retention configuration was changed in external services by this revision.
