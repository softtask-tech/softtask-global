# softtask.co production brief

Status: working brief, 14 September 2026. Public-facing source only; internal company records and publication approvals remain outside this repository.

## Scope and identity

Build a new global corporate website for softtask.co. Cloudflare is the selected hosting provider. Use the approved forest-green direction, strong readable typography, meaningful photography, actual product screenshots and original explanatory diagrams. Use varied editorial layouts and restrained purposeful interaction. No further standalone design demos are requested.

Other corporate websites have separate repositories and independent visual designs, navigation and country content. Keep a recognisable Soft Task identity without duplicating the global site's layouts.

## Proposed navigation and pages

- Home: concrete positioning, capabilities, approved evidence, products, useful insight and enquiry.
- Services: software engineering, AI and automation, data engineering, cloud and infrastructure, data centres, and blockchain engineering. Validate active scope before publication.
- Industries: publish only where there is substantive supported content.
- Products: Tubblor, Regulix One and Kytheos, distinguishing available features from roadmap items.
- Insights: technical explainers and research with sources, dates and review ownership.
- Company: overview, approved leadership, delivery approach and locations.
- Careers: genuine vacancies or an accurately described expression-of-interest route.
- Contact: service selection and routing to monitored sales mailboxes.
- Policies: privacy, terms and cookie information matching actual operations.
- Work: add when publishable project evidence is available.

Cybersecurity and STRID belong under Kytheos; digital marketing belongs under Tubblor. Corporate portfolio links can explain these relationships without duplicating their service catalogues. Kytheos is rebuilt last.

## Required website functions

Contact forms need server-side validation, spam protection, reliable acceptance and notification handling, accessible errors and clear confirmation. Routing and recipients must be confirmed against active mailboxes.

Newsletter subscriptions need email confirmation, an unsubscribe mechanism, subscription preferences and a chosen delivery provider. Do not describe a subscription as active before provider acceptance and any required confirmation.

Cookie preferences need equally discoverable accept and reject choices for optional cookies, category controls and a persistent settings link. Connect choices to the tools actually installed and verify behaviour before and after consent changes. Select analytics before finalising categories and policy text.

Publishing needs structured article and service content, drafts, review ownership and a practical editing workflow. Choose the editing tool during implementation based on who maintains the site.

Include responsive navigation, useful error pages, page metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, social previews, image optimisation, keyboard accessibility, visible focus and reduced-motion support. Add search when content volume warrants it.

## Content and imagery rules

Company records collected from old websites are references, not automatically verified current facts. Resolve conflicting legal names and office addresses before publishing them. Saudi address can remain absent until supplied. Registration numbers remain internal by default; assess applicable disclosure obligations before launch.

Remove ISO claims. Publish client names, logos, project details and metrics only with evidence and permission. Do not disclose NDA-covered work. Use genuine team and work images where available; licensed illustrative images must not imply ownership of facilities. Product images must reflect actual availability.

Every service page should explain the problem, scope, deliverables, dependencies, delivery process and practical next step. Articles should answer specific questions with sources and technical detail, not repeat generic marketing claims.

## Implementation sequence

1. Finalise global navigation, service grouping, homepage copy and an image/evidence register.
2. Establish the production project and structured content model; evaluate Astro static output with selective interactive components for Cloudflare.
3. Build the real homepage, infrastructure service page and an article template in the selected direction.
4. Complete remaining approved content and integrate contact, newsletter, cookie controls and publishing.
5. Verify representative mobile and desktop pages, accessibility, performance, forms and consent behaviour.
6. Connect protected staging, then prepare production and the softtask.tech migration after launch checks.

Do not carry forward legacy URLs or layouts solely because they exist. Plan redirects against relevant old destinations and actual usage before cutover.
